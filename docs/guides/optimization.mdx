# Optimization

Quantum optimization is an emerging field that explores how quantum computing can be used to solve optimization problems, where the goal is to find the best solution among many possible alternatives. Applications include logistics and scheduling, portfolio optimization, resource allocation, and materials science.

Quantum optimization approaches include quantum approximate optimization algorithms (QAOA), quantum annealing, and hybrid quantum-classical methods that combine quantum processors with classical optimization techniques. Although quantum computing may offer advantages for certain optimization problems, identifying practical performance benefits over classical methods remains an active area of research
