  #### box

  <Function id="qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box" github="https://github.com/Qiskit/qiskit-paulice/tree/stable/0.3/qiskit_paulice/checked_circuit.py#L401-L434" signature="box(payload_layers=None, **kwargs)">
    Box [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit") while maintaining concurrent scheduling of payload layers.

    This method stratifies the entangling layers of the checked circuit into boxes such that the number of unique entangling layers is minimized. This is done by scheduling the entangling gates from the Pauli checks into boxes of their own, resulting in one unique layer per Pauli check in addition to the `payload_layers`.

    Scheduling check gates into boxes of their own is beneficial in that the number of unique entangling layers is minimized, but it comes at a cost of sub-optimal gate scheduling.

    **Parameters**

    *   **payload\_layers** ([*Iterable*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/collections.abc.html#collections.abc.Iterable)*\[*[*Iterable*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/collections.abc.html#collections.abc.Iterable)*\[*[*tuple*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/stdtypes.html#tuple)*\[*[*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)*,* [*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)*]]] | None*) – The unique entangling layers of the bare payload circuit. Each inner list contains the edges for one unique layer. Edges should not be repeated within the same layer, but may appear in multiple layers; each stratum of the boxed circuit is then consistent with (a subset of) one of these layers.
    *   **\*\*kwargs** – Overrides for [`generate_boxing_pass_manager()`](/docs/addons/samplomatic/api/auto/transpiler-generate-boxing-pass-manager#samplomatic.transpiler.generate_boxing_pass_manager "(in samplomatic)"). Defaults to the key-value pairs in [`BOXING_DEFAULTS`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.BOXING_DEFAULTS "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.BOXING_DEFAULTS").

    **Returns**

    [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit"), boxed and annotated.

    **Raises**

    *   [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – `payload_layers` does not describe this circuit’s payload gates.
    *   [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit") contains an instruction other than one- and two-qubit unitary gates, measurements, and barriers.

    **Return type**

    [*QuantumCircuit*](/docs/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.QuantumCircuit)
  </Function>

  #### dope

  <Function id="qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.dope" github="https://github.com/Qiskit/qiskit-paulice/tree/stable/0.3/qiskit_paulice/checked_circuit.py#L316-L399" signature="dope(num_sites=None, *, wires='all', angle=0.7853981633974483, seed=None, box=False, payload_layers=None, box_options=None)">
    Dope `self.circuit` with [`RZGate`](/docs/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.library.RZGate) rotations that commute with check stabilizers.

    **Parameters**

    *   **num\_sites** ([*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int) *| None*) – Number of [`RZGate`](/docs/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.library.RZGate) rotations to place in the circuit. `None` uses every possible location.

    *   **wires** ([*Literal*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/typing.html#typing.Literal)*\['all', 'after\_entangling', 'before\_entangling']*) –

        Which wires may hold a rotation:

        *   `"all"`: Dope every possible wire.
        *   `"after_entangling"`: Dope only wires directly after multi-qubit gates.
        *   `"before_entangling"`: Dope only wires directly before multi-qubit gates.

    *   **angle** ([*float*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#float) *| None*) – Rotation angle for each inserted [`RZGate`](/docs/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.library.RZGate). `None` instead gives each rotation its own parameter from a [`ParameterVector`](/docs/api/qiskit/qiskit.circuit.ParameterVector) named `dope`. The rotation on the returned `doped_wires[i]` gets `dope[i]`, so a list of angles binds in `doped_wires` order.

    *   **seed** ([*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)  *|*[*Generator*](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/random/generator.html#numpy.random.Generator) *| None*) – Seed or generator for the random site selection.

    *   **box** ([*bool*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#bool)) – Whether to box the doped circuit, as [`box()`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box") boxes [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit").

    *   **payload\_layers** ([*Iterable*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/collections.abc.html#collections.abc.Iterable)*\[*[*Iterable*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/collections.abc.html#collections.abc.Iterable)*\[*[*tuple*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/stdtypes.html#tuple)*\[*[*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)*,* [*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)*]]] | None*) – The `payload_layers` argument of [`box()`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box"). Only used with `box=True`.

    *   **box\_options** ([*Mapping*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/collections.abc.html#collections.abc.Mapping)*\[*[*str*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/stdtypes.html#str)*,* [*Any*](https://docs.python.org/3/library/typing.html#typing.Any)*] | None*) – Overrides for [`generate_boxing_pass_manager()`](/docs/addons/samplomatic/api/auto/transpiler-generate-boxing-pass-manager#samplomatic.transpiler.generate_boxing_pass_manager "(in samplomatic)"), as the `**kwargs` of [`box()`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box"). Only used with `box=True`.

    **Returns**

    a copy of [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit") with the rotations inserted, and the [`Wire`](qiskit-paulice-wire#wire "qiskit_paulice.wire.Wire") holding each rotation, in circuit order and with instruction indices into [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit"). The checks and classical bits are unchanged, so [`get_postselection_method()`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.get_postselection_method "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.get_postselection_method") applies to the doped circuit’s results.

    **Return type**

    `(doped_circuit, doped_wires)`

    **Raises**

    *   [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit") contains a non-Clifford instruction.
    *   [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – `wires` is not one of the allowed values.
    *   [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – `payload_layers` or `box_options` is given without `box=True`, or [`box()`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.box") rejects the doped circuit.
    *   [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – `num_sites` is negative, larger than the number of valid sites, or that many sites could not be drawn at random from them.

    **Example**

    Dope a one-qubit circuit with parametrized rotations, then bind their angles:

    ```python
    import numpy as np
    from qiskit import QuantumCircuit
    from qiskit_paulice import CheckedCircuit

    circuit = QuantumCircuit(1)
    circuit.h(0)
    circuit.h(0)
    circuit.h(0)
    circuit.measure_all()

    doped_circuit, doped_wires = CheckedCircuit(circuit).dope(angle=None)
    print(doped_wires)
    # (Wire(qubit=0, after_instruction=0), Wire(qubit=0, after_instruction=1))
    # dope[0] follows the first H gate, and dope[1] follows the second.

    bound = doped_circuit.assign_parameters([np.pi / 4, np.pi / 8])
    ```

    **References**

    S. Martiel et al., *Sampling hard circuits with verifiably high fidelity*, [arXiv:2607.25941](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.25941).
  </Function>

  #### estimate\_fault\_rates

  <Function id="qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.estimate_fault_rates" github="https://github.com/Qiskit/qiskit-paulice/tree/stable/0.3/qiskit_paulice/checked_circuit.py#L224-L314" signature="estimate_fault_rates(noise_model, shots=100000, seed=None)">
    Estimate acceptance, harmless-fault, logical-error, and check trigger rates.

    One noisy Monte Carlo sampling under `noise_model` yields all rates. A shot is *accepted* if all check syndromes are $0$. An error is *harmless* if it is non-identity yet backpropagates to a diagonal Pauli on the input, acting as a global phase on $|0^n\rangle$. A **logical error** is a sample corrupted by a logical error that evaded the checks. The *check trigger rate* is the probability that a given check flags a non-zero syndrome. All fault rates are with respect to all shots taken.

    **Parameters**

    *   **noise\_model** ([*NoiseModel*](qiskit-paulice-noise-models#noisemodel "qiskit_paulice.noise_models.NoiseModel")) – Noise to apply during Monte Carlo sampling.
    *   **shots** ([*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)) – Number of fault configurations to sample.
    *   **seed** ([*int*](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#int)  *|*[*Generator*](https://numpy.org/doc/stable/reference/random/generator.html#numpy.random.Generator) *| None*) – Seed or generator for the fault sampling.

    **Returns**

    The estimated fault rates with their standard errors.

    **Raises**

    [**ValueError**](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/exceptions.html#ValueError) – The noise model is empty or unsupported, or [`circuit`](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.CheckedCircuit.circuit") contains a non-Clifford instruction.

    **Return type**

    [*FaultRates*](#qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.FaultRates "qiskit_paulice.checked_circuit.FaultRates")
  </Function>
