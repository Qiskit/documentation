## Passive linear optics

A number-conserving transformation of the mode operators, generated by $b^\dagger_i b_j$. Beamsplitters and phase shifters are the two-mode and single-mode special cases, and together they generate any $U(M)$.

### Synthesis methods

How an [`Evolution`](/docs/api/qiskit-bosons/circuit-library-evolution#qiskit_bosons.circuit.library.Evolution "qiskit_bosons.circuit.library.Evolution") gate gets decomposed in bosonic space is configurable through its `synthesis` argument. The available methods live in the [`synthesis`](circuit-library-synthesis#module-qiskit_bosons.circuit.library.synthesis "qiskit_bosons.circuit.library.synthesis") module.
