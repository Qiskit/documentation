# FermionicGivensDecomposition

<Class id="qiskit_fermions.circuit.library.synthesis.FermionicGivensDecomposition" isDedicatedPage={true} signature="FermionicGivensDecomposition(*, atol=1e-12)" modifiers="class">
  Bases: [`object`](https://docs.python.org/3/builtins/functions.html#object)

  The decomposition of an orbital rotation into Givens rotations and phases.

  The unitary $U$ of the [`OrbitalRotation`](circuit-library-orbital-rotation "qiskit_fermions.circuit.library.OrbitalRotation") is factorized with [`givens_decomposition()`](linalg-givens-decomposition "qiskit_fermions.linalg.givens_decomposition") into at most $n(n-1)/2$ rotations between *adjacent* modes plus $n$ single-mode phases, the rectangular mesh of Clements et al.   <span id="id1" />[\[1\]](#id2). Every factor $F$ is itself an orbital rotation, whose many-body unitary is $e^{-i H_F}$ for the Hermitian generator $H_F = \sum_{ij} i \log(F)_{ij} \, a^\dagger_i a_j$. It is therefore emitted as an [`Evolution`](circuit-library-evolution "qiskit_fermions.circuit.library.Evolution") gate at unit time on the one or two modes it acts on.

  The emitted gates are `atomic`, and the operator of each holds a single group. The terms of a two-mode generator, $a^\dagger_0 a_1$ and $a^\dagger_1 a_0$, do not commute with each other, so splitting it up any further would only approximate the factor: the fermion-to-qubit stage maps and synthesizes it whole. Under Jordan-Wigner, the generator of a rotation between adjacent modes is a hop between adjacent qubits, which the qubit stage can recognize (see [`HopEvolutionSynthesis`](transpiler-passes-synthesis-hop-evolution-synthesis "qiskit_fermions.transpiler.passes.synthesis.HopEvolutionSynthesis")).

  Because $U \mapsto \exp(\sum_{ij} \log(U)_{ij} a^\dagger_i a_j)$ is a group homomorphism, the product of the factors equals the full rotation exactly. The decomposition itself therefore adds no error: only the synthesis of the individual factors can.

  <span id="id2" />

  \[[1](#id1)]

  W. R. Clements et al., Optimal design for universal multiport interferometers, Optica 3, 1460 (2016), [arXiv:1603.08788](https://arxiv.org/abs/1603.08788).
