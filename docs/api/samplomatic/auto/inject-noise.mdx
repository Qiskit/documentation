# samplomatic.InjectNoise

<Class id="samplomatic.InjectNoise" isDedicatedPage={true} github="https://github.com/Qiskit/samplomatic/tree/main/samplomatic/annotations/inject_noise.py#L33-L77" signature="samplomatic.InjectNoise(ref: str, modifier_ref: str = '', site: InjectionSite | Literal['before', 'after'] = 'after')" modifiers="class">
  Directive to inject noise into a `box` instruction.

  The resulting [`Samplex`](samplex-samplex "samplomatic.samplex.Samplex") built from a circuit with a box with this annotation requires a [`qiskit.quantum_info.PauliLindbladMap`](/docs/api/qiskit/qiskit.quantum_info.PauliLindbladMap) with `ref` to at sample time. The qubits of the map are indexed in physical qubit order, in other words, the order of the qubits in the outer-most circuit, restricted to those used by the box.

  **Parameters**

  *   **ref** – A unique identifier of the Pauli Lindblad map from which to inject noise.
  *   **modifier\_ref** – A unique identifier for modifiers to apply to the Pauli Lindblad map before injection.
  *   **site** – Whether to inject the noise before or after the hard content of the box.
