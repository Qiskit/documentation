## Operators and their structure

A bosonic operator is a sum of ordered products of creation and annihilation operators, and a mode can appear more than once in a term. Bosonic operators can also tag their terms with group indices, which later stages use to split a time evolution along physically meaningful pieces. Mappers turn an operator into a qubit operator under a truncation and an encoding. Start here if you are building a Hamiltonian.
