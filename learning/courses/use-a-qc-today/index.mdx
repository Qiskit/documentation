# Overview

!["Hero image"](/learning/images/courses/use-a-qc-today/hero.svg)


Welcome to *Use a quantum computer today*! In the course, you'll start using a quantum computer immediately, and learn the basic principles of quantum computing along the way.

This course is designed for the curious and the busy. You don't need a physics degree or years of programming experience. If you can follow along with a notebook and click "Run," you're ready.

## Exam

To earn your badge for the "Using a quantum computer" course, take the exam at IBM&reg; Training. This exam is intended to be taken after reading the lessons in this course. After you pass the exam, you will be notified by Credly that you have earned a badge.
