# Overview

This course explores some of the most promising applications of quantum computing, including Hamiltonian simulation, optimization, machine learning, and partial differential equations. Each application is introduced through its theoretical motivation and then explored through hands-on case studies. We examine how real-world problems are mapped to quantum computers, which quantum techniques they use, and what evidence is needed to assess their potential.
